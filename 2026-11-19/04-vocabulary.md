## 5-Minute Self-Edit Checklist (Before Submitting)

1. **`allow / enable + object + TO + infinitive`:** Verify you included both the object and `to` (e.g., *"Stablecoins enable **citizens to hedge** against inflation"*).
2. **Verb tense & 3rd-person `-s` consistency:** Check singular subjects in the present tense (*"inflation erodes"*, *"regulation absorbs"*).
3. **No standalone `Because` fragments:** Connect every `because` clause to its main clause.
4. **Past counterfactual conditionals:** Use `If + had + past participle` in the condition clause, and avoid double negatives (`would never have existed`, never `never would not have existed`).
